Hot Flash Kitchen Support

Food ideas for how you feel today, and easy ways for your partner to take care of mealtimes.

Contact: hotflashkitchenapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

Is my information private? Yes. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The only things that go online are what's needed to link your phones and check your plan: anonymous IDs, the link, the invite code, your partner's first name on their join request (until you answer), and your plan status. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I set up the app for my partner? Your partner installs Hot Flash Kitchen on their own iPhone and chooses "I'm the partner" when it asks who's using the phone. They enter their name and number, yours, and the invite code you texted them (it's also on your Profile). Then you tap Yes to confirm it's them.

We picked the wrong side on this phone. On the Profile tab, find "This phone" and tap Change. It's in the same place on both sides.

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Hot Flash Kitchen.

I bought a plan but it isn't showing. Tap "Restore purchases" in the app, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do subscriptions work? The partner plan (both phones) is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year; Just me (one phone) is $6.99 a month or $69.99 a year. There's no free trial: your first check-in and its three ideas are free once, as a taste. After that, a plan unlocks the app. Profile and the Guide are always open, including Restore purchases, unlinking, and the Terms and Privacy Policy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and a subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

My partner and I both pay. How do we share one plan? One partner plan covers both linked phones, and either of you can buy it. If you have the Just me plan, switch to the partner plan on your Profile: Apple swaps it over, so you never pay twice. If you both end up paying, the app tells the one who should cancel and opens their Apple subscription settings. Canceling stops the renewal; the plan stays active until the period ends. If you were charged twice, you can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does my partner need an iPhone? For the partner plan, yes, for now: Partner Mode is iPhone only at launch, and Android is coming. You can still use the app on your own with the Just me plan, and text your partner from it on any phone.

One of us changed phone numbers. Your link and your plan don't change. Each phone keeps both numbers, so update it in both places: the person with the new number edits their Me card on Profile, and the other person edits the Partner card on theirs, so the "Text" buttons reach the new number.

One of us got a new iPhone. If you moved everything with an iCloud backup or Quick Start, the app comes back as it was. On a fresh setup, the app starts over and your history stays on the old phone (it never goes online). Then relink: the person still on their old phone taps Unlink on Profile, and she makes a fresh code for the new phone. If you paid for the plan, tap Restore purchases on your new phone. Your plan comes back without paying again.

Can I turn reminders off? Yes, on Profile in the app, or in your phone's Settings under Notifications.

Is this medical advice? No. The app offers food ideas. For anything about your health, talk to your clinician.
