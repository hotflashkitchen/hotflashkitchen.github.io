Hot Flash Kitchen Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the Hot Flash Kitchen app ("the app", "we", "us") and explains what it does with information. The short version: what you enter stays on your phone. Only what's needed to link two phones and to check your plan goes online. We don't run ads, we don't sell or share your information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter or do in the app is stored only on your own phone and is never sent to us or anyone else, including:

  • your name, phone number (if you add a partner), pronouns, food preferences, and the city or ZIP code you type in
  • your partner's name, pronouns and phone number, if you add them
  • how you feel each day, the meals you pick, cook, save, order or eat out, and your ratings
  • your reminder settings

The app doesn't use your phone's location services, contacts, camera, microphone or health data. Your phone's own backups (such as iCloud Backup) include the app's data if you have them turned on, under Apple's terms.

What goes online, and why

Only the items below leave your phone, and only to make two things work: linking your phone with your partner's, and checking whether your plan is active.

1. Linking two phones: Google Firebase

  • An anonymous ID for each phone that links. It's a random code, not tied to your name, email or phone number.
  • The link record: the two phones' anonymous IDs and when they were linked.
  • The invite code: when it was made, and whether it's been used. Each code works once and expires after 48 hours.
  • Your partner's first name, briefly. When your partner enters your code, the first name they type goes with their request so you can confirm it's them. It's deleted as soon as you answer, or after 48 hours if you don't.
  • Technical data Firebase logs automatically: the phone's IP address and basic device details (such as the operating system version), which Firebase keeps for a few weeks to protect against misuse.

2. Your plan: RevenueCat

  • An anonymous ID for your plan (a random code; linked phones share one so a partner plan can cover both).
  • Your purchase history for this app, as Apple reports it: which plan, when it started and renews, the price and store country, and whether it's active.
  • Technical data RevenueCat's service receives when checking your plan: the app version, device type, operating system version and IP address.

3. Payment: Apple Subscriptions are sold, billed and refunded by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

That's everything. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no cookies.

These companies process this information for us as service providers, under their own terms: Firebase privacy, RevenueCat privacy, Apple privacy.

How we use it

Only to link your phone with your partner's, let you confirm who's joining, keep the link working, check and restore your plan, and protect the service from misuse. We don't use it for advertising, profiling or anything else, and we don't sell or share it.

When information is shared

Only with the service providers above, so the app can work. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the app (in which case this policy still applies to what was collected under it).

How long it's kept

  • The link record: until either person unlinks.
  • Invite codes: until used, canceled, or 48 hours pass.
  • Your partner's first name on a request: until you answer, or 48 hours at most.
  • Firebase's technical logs: a few weeks.
  • Purchase history at RevenueCat: while you have a plan, and afterward as needed for records, unless you ask us to delete it. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its terms.

Links you tap and texts you send

  • Takeout and eating-out ideas can open Yelp, Google Maps, Uber Eats, DoorDash or OpenTable. When you tap one, that service receives the search: the cuisine and the city or ZIP code you entered. What happens there is covered by that service's privacy policy.
  • The "Text" buttons open your own Messages app with a message ready to send. The app doesn't send anything itself, and we never see your messages.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone. No server is involved. Turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Deleting your information

  • Everything on your phone: delete the app.
  • The link: tap Unlink on your Profile (or "Unlink and delete" on the partner's side). This deletes the link record, any invite code and that phone's anonymous ID from Firebase right away, and disconnects the other phone.
  • Purchase history at RevenueCat: email hotflashkitchenapp@gmail.com and we'll delete it. This doesn't cancel a subscription; to cancel, go to your Apple Account settings.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including California and other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what information we have, get a copy, correct it, or have it deleted. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising. To make a request, email hotflashkitchenapp@gmail.com. Because we hold only anonymous IDs, we may ask for a detail that helps us find your records, such as the date you subscribed. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Do Not Track: the app doesn't track you across apps or websites, so there's nothing for a Do Not Track signal to turn off.

Security

Information that goes online is sent encrypted (HTTPS). The link records are protected so each phone can reach only its own household's record. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what's online as small as possible so there's little to protect.

Where it's processed

Firebase and RevenueCat may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. The app is offered in the United States only.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the link feature, email us and we'll delete it.

Not medical advice

The app offers food ideas. It is not medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your clinician about your health.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new policy here. If a change means new information goes online, we'll say so in the app before it happens.
